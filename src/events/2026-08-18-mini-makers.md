A special hands-on session for younger crafters. Kids ages 1.5-5 can create their own spoon puppet using markers, pipe cleaners, yarn, stickers and other materials. This session is drop-in style so come between 9:30-10:30 and and leave when your creation is finished. Adults need to stay with their child during this event.
